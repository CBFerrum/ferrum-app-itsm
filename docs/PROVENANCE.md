# Provenance

This app's design and code are its own. No code was copied from another project.

- Its shape follows the practices ITIL describes and IT service desks use: configuration items and
  their relations, a service catalog, incidents prioritised by an impact and urgency matrix,
  problems and known errors, and changes with approval, freezes and back-out plans.
- The priority matrix and the targets (4, 8, 24 and 72 hours) are common defaults, not taken from
  any product.
